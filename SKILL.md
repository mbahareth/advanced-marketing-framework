# Advanced Marketing Framework

This skill delivers psychology-driven marketing and copywriting that adapts core messaging to the target audience's income level. It combines income-segmented persuasion with a five-pillar content methodology for maximum engagement and conversion.

## When to Use

- Creating or rewriting marketing copy, landing pages, emails, ads, or website content.
- Analyzing an existing website or campaign for persuasive effectiveness.
- Developing brand messaging or positioning for a specific audience segment.
- Generating promotional materials that must work in both English and Arabic.

## Core Framework: Income-Segmented Persuasion

Always adapt the primary message based on the target income bracket:

| Segment | Core Focus | Key Triggers | Psychological Driver |
|---------|------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **Low-Income** | Promises | Guarantees, Testimonials, Tactics, Goals | Security, risk reduction, immediate practical solutions |
| **Middle-Income** | Assets | ROI, Efficiency, Status, Long-term Value | Aspiration, smart investment, upward mobility |
| **High-Income** | Philosophy | Insight, Ideas, Leadership, Identity | Self-actualization, legacy, worldview reinforcement |

For full details, examples, tone guidelines, and common mistakes, read `references/income_framework.md`.

## Content Generation Methodology (Five Pillars)

Every piece of content or analysis MUST apply all five pillars:

1. **Steve Jobs Style** — Extreme clarity, grand vision, enemy-vs-hero narrative.
2. **NLP Techniques** — Sensory language, presuppositions, audience mirroring.
3. **Modern Marketing Frameworks** — Jobs-to-be-Done + StoryBrand (customer as hero, brand as guide).
4. **The Nudge** — Choice architecture, social proof, reduced friction.
5. **Cliffhangers** — Open loops that drive continued engagement.

For concrete techniques, language patterns, and application examples, read `references/content_methodology.md`.

## Workflow

1. **Identify the Target Segment**
   - Determine primary income level (Low / Middle / High) from context, product price, audience description, or explicit request.
   - If unclear, ask or default to Middle and note the assumption.

2. **Select the Angle**
   - Choose the matching core focus: Promises, Assets, or Philosophy.
   - Read the corresponding section in `references/income_framework.md`.

3. **Draft Using the Five Pillars**
   - Apply Jobs style, NLP, JTBD/StoryBrand, Nudge, and open loops.
   - Read `references/content_methodology.md` before drafting.

4. **Produce Dual-Language Output**
   - Deliver the final content in both English and Arabic (Modern Standard Arabic / Fus'ha).
   - The Arabic version must carry the same emotional, psychological, and persuasive weight — not a literal translation.
   - Structure clearly (headings, bullets, or parallel sections).

5. **Include Assets When Analyzing**
   - When analyzing or recreating a specific site or campaign, reference all necessary photos, files, links, and visual assets.

6. **Final Review**
   - Confirm segment alignment, all five pillars present, dual-language quality, and at least one open loop.

## Output Requirements

- Always produce **both English and Arabic**.
- Arabic must match emotional intensity and persuasion power.
- Use clear structure. Prefer short, powerful sentences in the Jobs style.
- End major sections with an open loop or compelling question.
- For website analyses, list or embed references to original assets.
